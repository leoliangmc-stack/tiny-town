# Ideas

Anything that is not part of the current phase goes here. Nothing on this list may be built
without first moving it into `SPEC.md` and `PHASES.md`.

- A Playwright capture script that seeks the world to a given hour and screenshots it at
  desktop and portrait sizes. It was used to review the Phase 1 lighting; worth keeping
  as a repo script once Phase 7 needs the 20x time-lapse GIF, if the extra dev
  dependency is acceptable in CI.
- Garden paths from each front door to the pavement, to explain the route citizens walk
  across the grass.
- From `DESIGN.md`, not scheduled for v1: a small fountain in the park, a basketball hoop
  at the school, a chalkboard and parasols at the cafe, washing lines, school bags, taxis,
  puddles and drips from eaves in rain, wet-road reflections of car lights, a vegetable
  patch.
